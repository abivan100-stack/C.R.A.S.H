## Cross-cutting themes

1. **The impact numbers are the weakest link.** Findings #1 / #17 / #43 are the same root cause in
   three surfaces (page cards, compare cards, both PDFs): a severity-weighted score is printed with
   an incident-count noun. It is the one figure a judge is most likely to interrogate, and the
   arithmetic does not support the sentence.
2. **Capped values presented as counts** (#2, #55): the "Emerging hotspots" KPI is a `slice`, not a count.
3. **Hardcoded window lengths** (#9, #17, #45): "24 mo" / "18 mo" are typed into strings rather than
   derived, and go stale the moment a citizen report extends the data range.
4. **Four independent copies of the night / hour / dow rule** (#36, #56 plus `engine.js` and `utils.js`).
5. **Three different datasets across three pages** (#5, #24): the map, Analytics and Compare each
   assemble their own record set, so the same headline can differ between tabs.
